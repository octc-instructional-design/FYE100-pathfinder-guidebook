Mile Marker

Mile Marker #2: My Support Team

The assignment, with support, and a form you can fill in and save.

About 20 minutes 8 of 9 in this module

No one completes a journey alone.

This Mile Marker is about the people and resources around you, and how you'll use them on purpose. You'll come back to it when you build your capstone project.

What you'll do

  1. Before you start, find your Success Coach. Your AI Chat, Who Has Your Back?, also helps with Question 1.
    • Lesson 2.2: Who's Your Success Coach? (you'll need your coach's name for Question 1)
  2. Answer three questions in the form below.
  3. Save as PDF and upload it to the Mile Marker #2 assignment in Blackboard.

●○AI use: AI-supported

You may use AI as a thinking partner to explore your ideas. Work with what it gives you, then write your responses in your own voice. AI isn't a substitute for people: if you're dealing with stress or anything personal, reach out to your instructor, your Success Coach, or the OCTC Counseling Center. See the AI Course Policy in the syllabus and Lesson 1.4: AI use labels.

Optional prompt to try: "Ask me questions to help me identify the people and resources in my life that support my success as a college student, and how I can use them more intentionally this semester." Use what's useful. Your final responses must be in your own words.

Your Mile Marker

Type your answers in the boxes. Your typing is saved in this browser as you go, so you can come back later on the same device. Nothing is sent anywhere until you upload it.

Mile Marker #2: My Support Team

Enter your name before saving.

Question 1Three People at OCTC

Start with your Success Coach. At least two of your three should work at OCTC: an instructor, a tutor, a peer mentor, or someone in an office like TRIO, Counseling, or the Library. A classmate can be your third.

A strong answer

Names three people at OCTC, at least two who work here, including your Success Coach by name, and says how each one can help. A person, not an office. If you don't know a name yet, say who they are and where you'd find them.

1. Your Success Coach

2. A second person at OCTC

3. A third person at OCTC (a classmate can count)

Look back at: Lesson 2.2: Who's Your Success Coach?; Lesson 2.3: OCTC support resources

Question 2Using Your Network

Not just "I'll reach out when I need help," but when, how, and for what.

To get you thinking (no separate answer needed)
  • Which connections do you want to strengthen this semester?
A strong answer

Describes two specific ways you'll use your network, each with when, how, and for what you'll reach out.

Look back at: Lesson 2.3: Four tips for confident help-seeking

Question 3One New Resource

Choose one you haven't used yet, such as the Teaching & Learning Center (tutoring), the Counseling Center, the Library, Accessibility Services, TRIO, Career Services, Student Life and clubs, or Financial Aid.

A strong answer

Names a resource you haven't used yet, explains how it connects to your goals, and names one concrete next step.

Look back at: Lesson 2.3: OCTC support resources

In the print window, choose Save as PDF (or Microsoft Print to PDF) as the printer. The file name will start with "Mile Marker #2".

Submit it

Upload your PDF to the Mile Marker #2 assignment in Blackboard. Check Blackboard for the due date.

In an in-person class? Your instructor may give you a printed copy. Fill it in by hand, then upload a photo or scan.

How it's scored: full credit means every prompt is answered, and each answer is about you, with at least one real detail. See How Mile Markers are scored.
