Mile Marker #11: My Community Impact

The assignment, with support, and a form you can fill in and save.

About 15 minutes 6 of 7 in this module

Your community is bigger than you think, and you're already part of how it works.

This module asked you to notice the communities you're already part of, and to look at how each one works, including where you have influence. This Mile Marker asks you to put that into words, then name the four skills your STAR(T) Stories will show. Keep it short and honest.

What you'll do

  1. Before you start, fill in your Community Snapshot.
    • Community Snapshot (it has most of what you need for Questions 1 to 3)
  2. Answer four questions in the form below.
  3. Save as PDF and upload it to the Mile Marker #11 assignment in Blackboard.

●○AI use: AI-supported

You may use AI as a thinking partner to make your reflection more specific. Your final responses must be in your own words. Leave out your full name and other identifying details. See the AI Course Policy in the syllabus and Lesson 1.4: AI use labels.

Optional prompt to try: "I'm working on a short reflection about my community impact for a college course. Here's what I have so far for where I show up, how I make a difference, and how I want to grow that going forward: [paste your notes]. Can you ask me a few questions to help me make this more specific?" Use what's useful. Your final responses must be in your own words.

Your Mile Marker

Type your answers in the boxes. Your typing is saved in this browser as you go, so you can come back later on the same device. Nothing is sent anywhere until you upload it.

Part I: Your Community Impact

Question 1Where You Show Up

Think back to the community you worked with in this module, or pick another one that fits better now. Community word bank: family · friends · roommates · classmates · coworkers · neighbors · online groups or gaming communities · faith or cultural groups · customer service or caregiving roles · teams, clubs, or interest groups. Example: "My job. I work the front desk at a campus office. I'm usually the first person someone talks to, so I try to make sure people feel heard even when I can't fix their problem myself."

A strong answer

Names one real community, describes your role, and gives a specific example of how you already show up.

Look back at: Lesson 11.2: You're already in community

Question 2Your Influence, Named

Use what you practiced in this module: noticing the people, rules, and influence in a system. Small and real is exactly right. Impact word bank: being reliable · supporting others · encouraging someone · helping someone understand something · practicing patience · speaking up in discussions · leading in small ways · being a good roommate · showing up consistently · noticing what others miss.

A strong answer

Describes one specific action and the difference it makes, connected to something you noticed about how the community works.

Look back at: The shared kitchen example

Question 3Looking Ahead

As your circles grow (new classes, jobs, programs, organizations), how do you want to show up more intentionally?

A strong answer

Names one intention, connects it to a specific strength or value, and names a first step you could take.

Look back at: Lesson 11.3: Your offer and your boundary; Mile Marker #7

Part II: Your Four STAR(T) Skills

Question 4Choose Your Stories

Look back through your Mile Markers and your life this semester. For each skill, name the experience you plan to tell, in a phrase or a sentence. Use a different experience for each. Example: "Professionalism: the week my car broke down and I still got every assignment in by arranging rides and emailing my instructor ahead of time."

A strong answer

Names a real, specific experience for each skill, uses a different experience for each, and explains why you chose your fourth skill.

Look back at: Prompts for every skill in the STAR(T) Stories Guide

In the print window, choose Save as PDF (or Microsoft Print to PDF) as the printer. The file name will start with "Mile Marker #11".

Submit it

Upload your PDF to the Mile Marker #11 assignment in Blackboard. Check Blackboard for the due date.

In an in-person class? Your instructor may give you a printed copy. Fill it in by hand, then upload a photo or scan.

How it's scored: full credit means every prompt is answered, and each answer is about you, with at least one real detail. See How Mile Markers are scored.
