Rather work on paper, or want a copy that doesn't depend on your browser? It's all here.

Each printable is the same task as its page in the book, so use one or the other, not both. Print it and fill it in by hand, or type into it and save it. Either way, you submit it in Blackboard the same way.

Using a printable

  • On paper. Print it, fill it in by hand, then upload a clear photo or scan to the matching assignment in Blackboard.
  • On a computer. Open the PDF, type into the boxes, and save it. Then upload the PDF to Blackboard.
  • Get the newest copy. Download from this page each time rather than reusing an old file. Printables are updated when their page in the book changes.
Pathfinder Tip

Typing into a PDF? Open it in a PDF reader such as Adobe Acrobat Reader or Microsoft Edge. Some phone previews don't let you type in the boxes.
