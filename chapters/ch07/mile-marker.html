Mile Marker #7: My Strengths Snapshot

The assignment, with support, and a form you can fill in and save.

About 20 minutes 6 of 7 in this module

Who you are at your best, and what that means for where you're headed.

This Mile Marker asks you to do something with your HIGH5 and Values Card Sort results: reflect on what they mean for how you learn, how you work with others, and where you might be headed. There are no good or bad strengths, only honest reflection.

What you'll do

  1. Before you start, take both assessments. The rest of this Mile Marker uses your results.
    • Lesson 7.3: Take HIGH5 (your Top 5, saved in My HIGH5 Results)
    • Lesson 7.2: The Values Card Sort (your values, saved in My Values Snapshot)
  2. Answer six questions in the form below.
  3. Save as PDF and upload it to the Mile Marker #7 assignment in Blackboard.

●○AI use: AI-supported

You may use AI as a thinking partner to explore your results. Work with what it gives you, then write your responses in your own voice. You can share your strength names, but not your name, where you live, or other personal details. AI doesn't know you. You do. See the AI Course Policy in the syllabus and Lesson 1.4: AI use labels.

Optional prompt to try: "Help me understand my HIGH5 strengths for a college reflection. My Top 5 strengths are: [paste your list]. Explain each one in everyday language, then ask me questions, one at a time, about where I have seen them in my own life." Use what's useful. Your final responses must be in your own words.

Your Mile Marker

Type your answers in the boxes. Your typing is saved in this browser as you go, so you can come back later on the same device. Nothing is sent anywhere until you upload it.

Mile Marker #7: My Strengths Snapshot

Enter your name before saving.

Question 1Your Top 5 HIGH5 Strengths

List them exactly as the assessment reports them, spelling and capitalization included.

A strong answer

Lists all five, in order, exactly as reported.

Look back at: My HIGH5 Results (your Top 5)

Question 2Accuracy Reflection

What do your results say about how you think, feel, or work?

To get you thinking (no separate answer needed)
  • In your own everyday words, what does each strength mean for how you think and work?
  • Which result do you want to understand better?
A strong answer

Explains your strengths in plain language, names the one that fits best and why, and is honest about what surprised you.

Look back at: What your results actually mean; My HIGH5 Results (your baseline)

Question 3Strengths in Action: Academics

Be concrete: not "study more," but how your particular strengths change how you approach studying, class, or assignments.

A strong answer

Names three concrete ways, each tied to a specific strength, and describes how one strength shows up with others.

Look back at: Lesson 4.2: Study strategies that work

Question 4Strengths and Values in Action: Career Direction

How might your strengths and values connect to a future field, role, or work environment?

A strong answer

Names one or two work environments that fit your strengths and connects at least one of your values to the direction you're exploring.

Look back at: My Values Snapshot (your values); Mile Marker #6: the direction you named

Question 5One Blind Spot Worth Watching

Every strength has an overuse pattern, something that works against you when it goes too far. Be honest, not hard on yourself.

A strong answer

Names one real blind spot, describes what it looks like in your life, and gives one strategy for managing it.

Look back at: Lesson 7.3: Every strength has a blind spot

Question 6Strengths-Based Goal
A strong answer

Is specific, names a strength, and describes something you could actually do in the next few weeks.

In the print window, choose Save as PDF (or Microsoft Print to PDF) as the printer. The file name will start with "Mile Marker #7".

Submit it

Upload your PDF to the Mile Marker #7 assignment in Blackboard. Check Blackboard for the due date.

In an in-person class? Your instructor may give you a printed copy. Fill it in by hand, then upload a photo or scan.

How it's scored: full credit means every prompt is answered, and each answer is about you, with at least one real detail. See How Mile Markers are scored.
