Mile Marker #10: My Collaboration Profile

The assignment, with support, and a form you can fill in and save.

About 15 minutes 8 of 9 in this module

How you work best with others, and how you plan to work even better.

This module you explored your 16Personalities results and what helps, and doesn't, when you're working with others. This Mile Marker asks you to put that into a short, honest profile. There's no "best" type for collaboration, only an honest answer and a vague one.

What you'll do

  1. Before you start, take the 16Personalities assessment and fill in your Collaboration Snapshot.
    • Lesson 10.3: Take the 16Personalities assessment (your type, for Question 1)
    • Collaboration Snapshot (it has most of what you need)
  2. Answer three questions in the form below.
  3. Save as PDF and upload it to the Mile Marker #10 assignment in Blackboard.

●○AI use: AI-supported

You may use AI to help you organize your thoughts. Your final responses must be in your own words. AI doesn't know how you collaborate. You do. Leave out your full name and other identifying details. See the AI Course Policy in the syllabus and Lesson 1.4: AI use labels.

Optional prompt to try: "Help me organize a short Collaboration Profile for a college reflection assignment. My type, strengths, challenges, and what helps me are: [paste your notes]. Ask me one or two questions to make it more specific, then help me turn it into a clear summary." Use what's useful. Your final responses must be in your own words.

Your Mile Marker

Type your answers in the boxes. Your typing is saved in this browser as you go, so you can come back later on the same device. Nothing is sent anywhere until you upload it.

Enter your name before saving.

Question 1Your Type, Strengths & Challenges

Example: "INFJ-T: quiet planner who likes meaningful work. I'm an organized planner and a calm mediator, but I tend to avoid conflict and take on too much." Strengths word bank: clear communicator · supportive teammate · creative problem-solver · organized planner · calm mediator · detail-checker · energizer or motivator · deep thinker · flexible adapter · relationship-builder · big-picture visionary · dependable finisher. Challenges word bank: hard to get started · taking on too much · hard to speak up · avoiding conflict · coming across too blunt · overwhelmed by chaos · frustrated by unclear instructions · perfectionism · withdrawing under stress · trouble reading tone · over-controlling · resisting schedules.

A strong answer

Gives your type in your own words, and names two strengths and one or two challenges that sound like you, not like a quiz summary.

Look back at: Lesson 10.3: Your letters; Lesson 10.4: Strengths and struggles

Question 2Why, and What Helps

Think of your challenge as a tendency, not a flaw: needing structure, being introverted, processing fast or slow, group anxiety, or how your brain works. Sharing anything about neurodivergence is always optional. Strategies word bank: start with clear expectations · communicate my style · play to my strengths · ask clarifying questions · break tasks into pieces · use tools wisely · assume good intent · know my stress signals · use the 2-minute reset.

A strong answer

Explains the why behind one challenge, names three strategies that fit you, and connects one to a real experience.

Look back at: Lesson 10.4: Different brains; Lesson 10.5: Nine strategies

Question 3What You Need From Your Team

Examples: "I work best when tasks and deadlines are clearly divided." "I thrive when I can think before speaking."

A strong answer

Is one clear sentence that's specific, respectful, and something you could actually say to a real team.

Look back at: Strategy 2: Communicate your style

In the print window, choose Save as PDF (or Microsoft Print to PDF) as the printer. The file name will start with "Mile Marker #10".

Submit it

Upload your PDF to the Mile Marker #10 assignment in Blackboard. Check Blackboard for the due date.

In an in-person class? Your instructor may give you a printed copy. Fill it in by hand, then upload a photo or scan.

How it's scored: full credit means every prompt is answered, and each answer is about you, with at least one real detail. See How Mile Markers are scored.
