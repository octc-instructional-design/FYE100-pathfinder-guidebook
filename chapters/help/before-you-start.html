Before You Start

You might be wondering how college is going to fit into the life you already have. The students who came before you wondered that, too. Here's what they figured out along the way.

These notes come from the end-of-course reflections of students who finished FYE 100 last semester.

  1. Where do I even start?

    Stay organized, ask questions, and remember why you started.

    From a previous FYE student

  2. How am I going to keep up?

    Set aside time out of your day, it won't take up too much time:).

    From a previous FYE student

  3. How do I keep from falling behind?

    Do not procrastinate your work, you will feel 100% better and relieved the sooner you finish your work.

    From a previous FYE student

  4. What if I'm already overwhelmed?

    Remember that it's okay to take a break and breathe—not everyone is counting on you to be a top-notch student. Never be scared to ask for help or take a step back.

    From a previous FYE student

  5. What if I don't know how to do something?

    There is no shame in needing help or not knowing how to do something.

    From a previous FYE student, who also told future students to reach out even when pride makes it hard

More from last semester

  • If I don't know how to do something or need help on something or something needs to be fixed, it's my responsibility to be proactive and ask for help.

    From a previous FYE student

  • Find a schedule that works for you. You don't have to copy what your friends or classmates do.

    Adapted from a previous FYE student

  • Take it one day at a time and use all the resources you have available.

    Adapted from a previous FYE student

  • College is stressful. Lean on the people around you and talk about what's stressing you out. You don't have to handle it alone.

    Adapted from a previous FYE student

One thing to try this week

Pick one assignment and put two things on your calendar: when you'll start it, and when it's due. Leave enough time between the two that you could ask a question if you get stuck, and still have time to use the answer.
