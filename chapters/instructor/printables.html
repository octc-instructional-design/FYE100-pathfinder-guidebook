Part 7

Printables Library

Every printable for class, by module, plus other course documents.

19 of 21 in this guide

Everything you print for class, in one place.

Every printable is also open to students in the Printable Library, linked from the book's Contents page, so a student who misses class or prefers paper can get a copy without asking. The PDFs stay out of Blackboard. Each printable matches its page in the book, and when a page changes, its printable is rebuilt and replaced in both places.

Other course documents

Documents that also live in the Blackboard shell, kept here so you can print or share them from one place.

  • Discussion and Journal Guidelines (PDF), opens in a new tab: how discussions and journals work, for Module 1
  • Trail Tip: What's Actually Happening in That AI Chat? (PDF), opens in a new tab: a printable copy of the Module 2 Trail Tip
  • Practice source for the Is This Legit? AI Chat (PDF), opens in a new tab: a fictional research brief students evaluate in Module 4. It is not a real publication.

Using the printables

  • Students fill them in by hand in class, then upload a photo or scan to the matching Blackboard assignment.
  • Every printable is also a fillable PDF, so a student who misses class can download it from the student Printable Library and type into it instead.
  • Print from this page each semester rather than saving copies, so you always have the current version.
