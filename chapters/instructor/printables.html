Every printable for in-person classes, by module.

Everything you print for class, in one place.

These printables are for in-person sections. Online students use the fill-in forms in the book, so the PDFs stay out of Blackboard to avoid confusion. Each printable matches its page in the book, and when a page changes, its printable is rebuilt and replaced here.

Using the printables

  • Students fill them in by hand in class, then upload a photo or scan to the matching Blackboard assignment.
  • Every printable is also a fillable PDF, so a student who misses class can type into it instead.
  • Print from this page each semester rather than saving copies, so you always have the current version.
