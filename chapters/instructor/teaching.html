Teaching the Modules

Slides, coaching notes, and in-class ideas for every module.

7 of 9 in this guide

Everything you need to teach each module, in one place: slides, coaching notes, active learning ideas, and an in-class discussion.

Each module has its own page. The slides are PowerPoint files built from the same source as the book, with a talk track in the speaker notes, so they work for an in-person class or a recorded mini-lecture for online sections. Edit your copy however you like.

Public pages

Like the rest of the book, these pages are public. They hold no answer keys, scoring guidance, or points. Instructor keys stay in Blackboard.
