Mile Marker #8: My Career Snapshot

The assignment, with support, and a form you can fill in and save.

About 20 minutes 6 of 7 in this module

One career. Honest research. Your real assessment of the fit.

You've done the research. This Mile Marker asks you to make sense of it: not repeat it, but honestly assess what you found and what it means for you.

It's also how the college assesses Professionalism (#6), one of the course's focus skills. That doesn't mean it needs to sound polished. It needs to sound like you, and be specific and honest.

What you'll do

  1. Part 1: The Career Research. Complete the Career Research Guide. You'll upload it with your answers, and a submission without it is incomplete.
    • Career Research Guide (Part 1: all five sections and the synthesis, saved as a PDF)
  2. Part 2: The Questions. Answer three questions in the form below.
  3. Save as PDF and upload it to the Mile Marker #8 assignment in Blackboard.

●○AI use: AI-supported

You may use AI as a thinking partner to question your assumptions about a career. Get your facts from your research sources, not from AI: salary, outlook, and education requirements come from Career Coach, O*NET, and the Occupational Outlook Handbook. Leave your name and other personal details out of the prompt. See the AI Course Policy in the syllabus and Lesson 1.4: AI use labels.

Optional prompt to try: "I'm a college student exploring a career as a [career]. My top strengths include [two or three strengths], and I value [two or three values]. Ask me three hard questions that would help me test whether this career really fits me. Don't answer them for me." Use what's useful. Your final responses must be in your own words.

Part 2: The Questions

Type your answers in the boxes. Your typing is saved in this browser as you go, so you can come back later on the same device. Nothing is sent anywhere until you upload it.

Question 1What Did You Find?

Keep it brief. The guide has the detail. This is the headline version.

A strong answer

Covers all four: education, regional salary, outlook, and work environment, in your own words.

Look back at: Your guide, Sections 1 to 3

Question 2Does It Fit?

Connect your research to what you know about yourself from Modules 6 and 7.

A strong answer

Connects specific research findings to your strengths, your values, how you work best, and your pressure tolerance. Not just a list of your results.

Look back at: Your guide, Section 4; Mile Marker #7 and your Values Snapshot

Question 3What's Your Honest Take?
A strong answer

Names one alignment and one friction, asks one specific question, and names a person or resource that could help answer it.

Look back at: Your guide, Section 4 and the Synthesis

In the print window, choose Save as PDF (or Microsoft Print to PDF) as the printer. The file name will start with "Mile Marker #8".

Submit it

Upload your PDF to the Mile Marker #8 assignment in Blackboard. Check Blackboard for the due date.

In an in-person class? Your instructor may give you a printed copy. Fill it in by hand, then upload a photo or scan.

Upload your Career Research Guide in the same assignment. Both parts are needed for full credit.

How it's scored: full credit means every prompt is answered, and each answer is about you, with at least one real detail. See How Mile Markers are scored.
