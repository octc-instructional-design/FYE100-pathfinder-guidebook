Mile Marker #9: Think It Through

The assignment, with support, and a form you can fill in and save.

About 20 minutes 7 of 8 in this module

The thinking behind the thinking, and your first STAR(T) draft.

In this module you learned to spot thinking traps and use the 4C Check to slow down before reacting. You also saw how that same thinking makes a STAR(T) story more honest and specific. This Mile Marker has two parts: a look at your own thinking patterns, and your first STAR(T) draft, a real starting point for your capstone.

What you'll do

  1. Answer four questions in the form below.
  2. Save as PDF and upload it to the Mile Marker #9 assignment in Blackboard.

●○AI use: AI-supported

You may use AI as a thinking partner to ask you questions about your draft. It can't tell your story for you. Your responses must be in your own words and reflect your real experience. Leave out your full name, where you live, and other identifying details, including other people's names in your story. See the AI Course Policy in the syllabus and Lesson 1.4: AI use labels.

Optional prompt to try: "Help me think through a STAR(T) story for a college reflection assignment. Here's my draft: [paste]. Using the 4C Check (Clarity, Context, Credibility, Consequences), what's one part of my story that could be clearer, more specific, or more accurate? Ask me questions. Don't rewrite it for me." Use what's useful. Your final responses must be in your own words.

Your Mile Marker

Type your answers in the boxes. Your typing is saved in this browser as you go, so you can come back later on the same device. Nothing is sent anywhere until you upload it.

Part I: Know Your Pattern

Question 1Name Your Pattern and a Better Question

Which thinking trap or bias from this module feels most like you?

A strong answer

Names one pattern, gives a real example, offers a better question, and names one strategy for slowing down.

Look back at: Lesson 9.3: Biases; Thought traps

Part II: Your First STAR(T) Draft

Question 2Tell Your Story

Pick one real experience, from this semester or the recent past, and write it out using all five STAR(T) steps.

A strong answer

Includes all five steps, uses "I" in the Action, and sounds like you.

Look back at: Lesson 9.5: STAR(T) in five steps; Find Your Stories

Question 3Check It with the 4Cs

Reread your draft using the 4C Check and answer honestly. Then revise at least one part of your draft.

A strong answer

Answers honestly, and shows a real revision to at least one step.

Look back at: Lesson 9.5: Run your draft through the 4C Check

Question 4Name the Skill

You'll confirm your four capstone skills later, so for now, take your best guess.

A strong answer

Names one skill and connects it to something specific in your story.

Look back at: Your four capstone skills

In the print window, choose Save as PDF (or Microsoft Print to PDF) as the printer. The file name will start with "Mile Marker #9".

Submit it

Upload your PDF to the Mile Marker #9 assignment in Blackboard. Check Blackboard for the due date.

In an in-person class? Your instructor may give you a printed copy. Fill it in by hand, then upload a photo or scan.

How it's scored: full credit means every prompt is answered, and each answer is about you, with at least one real detail. See How Mile Markers are scored.
