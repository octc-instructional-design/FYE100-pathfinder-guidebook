Question 4One Goal, Two Steps

A goal is specific when someone else could tell whether you reached it. A step is concrete when you could put it on your calendar.

To get you thinking (no separate answer needed)
  • "Do better" isn't a goal. "Pass my math class" or "meet with my Success Coach twice" is a goal.
A strong answer

States one specific, realistic goal, names at least two concrete steps you'll take, and says what success will look like when you reach it.

Question 5Where You Stand with AI
To get you thinking (no separate answer needed)
  • AI can be obvious, like a chatbot, or hidden, like autocorrect or your recommended videos.
  • Maybe you're excited, unsure, skeptical, or you'd rather not use it at all. All of those are fair answers.
  • If you can, think of one way AI could support your learning without doing the learning for you.
A strong answer

Names at least two specific examples of AI in your daily life and describes honestly how you feel about using it for school. There's no right position here, only a clear one.
