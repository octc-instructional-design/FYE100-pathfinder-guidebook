Not a final decision. A starting point for the conversation with your Success Coach.

You don't have to have everything figured out. Four things are enough to walk into an advising appointment and have a real conversation: what your interest results told you, one direction worth exploring, what you need to ask, and what your next semester could realistically look like.

What you'll do

  1. Before you start, take the Career Coach assessment, and sketch your next semester in the Academic Planner.
    • Lesson 6.2: Take the Career Coach assessment (your results, for Question 1)
    • Academic Planner (your next semester, for Question 4)
  2. Answer four questions in the form below.
  3. Save as PDF and upload it to the Mile Marker #6 assignment in Blackboard.

Question 1Your Interest Results

Look at your Career Coach results (or the O*NET Interest Profiler).

To get you thinking (no separate answer needed)
  • What did the results suggest about the kind of work, or the people, you might thrive with?
A strong answer

Names your top interest types, says honestly whether they fit you, and connects your results to at least one career direction.

Look back at: Lesson 6.2: The six interest types; What to do with your results

Question 2One Direction Worth Exploring

Based on your results and your own instincts, choose one program or pathway at OCTC to learn more about.

A strong answer

Names one real OCTC program, explains why that credential type fits your goals and how it differs from the other options, and is honest about what you still need to find out.

Look back at: Lesson 6.3: What each credential means; Connecting your results to programs

Question 3Your Questions for Your Success Coach

Write five specific questions to bring to your advising appointment, most important first. Make them about your situation, your program interest, and your timeline. Include at least one about transfer, financial aid, or the order of your courses.

A strong answer

Lists five real, specific questions in priority order, with at least one about transfer, financial aid, or course order.

Look back at: Lesson 6.4: Questions to bring to your Success Coach

Question 4Your Next Semester

Sketch a realistic course load. It's a draft, and your Success Coach will help you finalize it.

A strong answer

Names specific courses and an approximate credit load, explains why it's realistic for your life, and mentions one thing about prerequisites or course order.
