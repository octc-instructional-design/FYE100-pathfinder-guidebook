Question 1Three People at OCTC

Start with your Success Coach. At least two of your three should work at OCTC: an instructor, a tutor, a peer mentor, or someone in an office like TRIO, Counseling, or the Library. A classmate can be your third.

A strong answer

Names three people at OCTC, at least two who work here, including your Success Coach by name, and says how each one can help. A person, not an office. If you don't know a name yet, say who they are and where you'd find them.

Look back at: Lesson 2.2: Who's Your Success Coach?; Lesson 2.3: OCTC support resources

Question 2Using Your Network

Not just "I'll reach out when I need help," but when, how, and for what.

To get you thinking (no separate answer needed)
  • Which connections do you want to strengthen this semester?
A strong answer

Describes two specific ways you'll use your network, each with when, how, and for what you'll reach out.

Look back at: Lesson 2.3: Four tips for confident help-seeking

Question 3One New Resource

Choose one you haven't used yet, such as the Teaching & Learning Center (tutoring), the Counseling Center, the Library, Accessibility Services, TRIO, Career Services, Student Life and clubs, or Financial Aid.

A strong answer

Names a resource you haven't used yet, explains how it connects to your goals, and names one concrete next step.
