All three stories are scored the same way: you get credit for each STAR(T) step you complete.

Written and recorded stories are held to the same standard. The table below shows what each step needs, and the fixes after it show how to make a story stronger.

How stories are scored

You get credit for each STAR(T) step you complete. A step counts when it's there and it's about you.

What each STAR(T) step needs to earn credit
StepIt counts when it...
SituationSets the scene: where, when, and what was going on.
TaskSays what you needed to do or solve.
ActionSays what you did, not only what "we" did.
ResultSays what happened because of what you did.
TransferNames where you'll use this skill next.

A response that isn't a true story about you, or doesn't use STAR(T), doesn't earn credit. Recorded stories are never scored on delivery, only on the story and its structure.

Three fixes that make a story stronger

Swap "we" for "I"

BeforeWe reorganized the schedule so everyone could make it.

AfterI asked each person for their work hours, built a new schedule around them, and sent it to the group that night.

Make the Result concrete

BeforeIt went really well.

AfterEveryone made the next three meetings, and we turned the project in a day early.

Name where the Transfer goes

BeforeThis will help me in my future career.

AfterIn clinicals next year I'll be coordinating with nurses on different shifts, and I now know to ask about everyone's constraints before I plan.

Submitting your stories

There are three separate My STAR(T) Story submission portals in Blackboard, one for each skill, in the Capstone module's CAPSTONE SUBMISSION PORTALS folder. The Fillable Forms folder there links to your three practice guides. You don't have to submit them all at once. Each portal is separate, so you can pace yourself. Check Blackboard for the due date.

[[tip]] AI and your stories

AI can help you brainstorm which experience to use, or ask you questions that help you remember details. It can't tell your story. Your stories have to be yours, in your own words and voice.
