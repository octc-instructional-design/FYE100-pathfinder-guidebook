Your capstone is four short, true stories. Each one shows a time you used one of the 10 Essential Skills.

You build them across the semester, and you turn in all four in Module 12.

Why this matters beyond FYE 100

Saying "I'm a hard worker" is easy. Showing it with a real story is what makes people believe you. You'll be asked for that kind of story again and again:

  • In job interviews. Many employers ask behavioral questions that start with "Tell me about a time when..." A STAR story is the answer they're listening for.
  • In applications. Scholarships, competitive programs like many health programs, and transfer applications often ask you to describe a real experience.
  • At work. Performance reviews, promotions, and references all come down to examples of what you actually did.

The skills you'll show are the 10 Essential Skills, the same ones every public college in Kentucky, including OCTC, builds its programs around. By the end of this course you'll have four true stories, practiced out loud, that you can adapt for any of those moments.

What you'll turn in

Four STAR(T) Stories, one for each skill below. Every story uses all five steps: Situation, Task, Action, Result, and Transfer.

Your four STAR(T) Stories
StoryThe skill it shows
1#5 Adaptability & Leadership: adjusting when things changed, or stepping up when it mattered.
2#6 Professionalism: following through, owning a mistake, or being someone others could count on.
3#9 Knowledge Application: a time something you learned met a moment you needed it.
4Your choice: any other Essential Skill. Start this story by naming the skill and why you chose it.

How to turn it in

  • [[tick]]Record it (strongly encouraged). Video or audio only, about 2 to 4 minutes. Recording is the best interview practice you can get, and it's never scored on delivery.
  • [[tick]]Or write it. Fill in that skill's practice guide so you cover all five steps, save it as a PDF, and upload it.
  • [[tick]]Mix and match. You can record some stories and write others.
  • [[tick]]One portal per story. In Blackboard, the Capstone module's CAPSTONE SUBMISSION PORTALS folder has one portal for each skill. Check Blackboard for the due date.

How it's scored

All four stories are scored the same way, on the story and its structure. A strong story has all five steps, and each one is specific to you: your Action says what you did, your Result names something concrete, and your Transfer names a real situation ahead. What Makes a Strong Story has the full scale and three fixes that move a story up.

Your stories have to be yours. AI can help you brainstorm or ask you questions, but it can't tell your story.

Where to start

Read The STAR(T) Framework for the five steps, then Find Your Stories for help choosing. Each practice guide walks you through one story, one step at a time.
