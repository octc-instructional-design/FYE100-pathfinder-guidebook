One career. Honest research. Your real assessment of the fit.

You've done the research. This Mile Marker asks you to make sense of it: not repeat it, but honestly assess what you found and what it means for you.

It's also how the college assesses Professionalism (#6), one of the course's focus skills. That doesn't mean it needs to sound polished. It needs to sound like you, and be specific and honest.

What you'll do

  1. Part 1: The Career Research. Complete the Career Research Guide. You'll upload it with your answers, and a submission without it is incomplete.
    • Career Research Guide (Part 1: all five sections and the synthesis, saved as a PDF)
  2. Part 2: The Questions. Answer three questions in the form below.
  3. Save as PDF and upload it to the Mile Marker #8 assignment in Blackboard.

●○AI use: AI-supported

You may use AI as a thinking partner to question your assumptions about a career. Get your facts from your research sources, not from AI: salary, outlook, and education requirements come from Career Coach, O*NET, and the Occupational Outlook Handbook. Leave your name and other personal details out of the prompt. See the AI Course Policy in the syllabus and Lesson 1.4: AI use labels.

Optional prompt to try: "I'm a college student exploring a career as a [career]. My top strengths include [two or three strengths], and I value [two or three values]. Ask me three hard questions that would help me test whether this career really fits me. Don't answer them for me." Use what's useful. Your final responses must be in your own words.
